# Faces: proportions

Placement and size of facial features relative to the head.

## Check eye line before features
- Beginners often place the eyes too high. Measure where the eye line falls on the head (top of skull to chin) in both drawing and photo; in a level, front-facing head it is near the halfway point.

## Compare the thirds
- Compare hairline-to-brow, brow-to-nose-base and nose-base-to-chin in both images. Name the section that differs most, and by roughly how much.

## Width between the eyes
- Compare the gap between the eyes to the width of one eye in both images. Eyes drawn too close or too far apart change the likeness quickly.
