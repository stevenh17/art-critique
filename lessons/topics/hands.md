# Hands

Size, finger lengths and joint placement of hands.

## Hand size against the face
- A hand from wrist to fingertip is roughly the length of the face from chin to hairline. Compare against the photo's hand-to-face ratio before calling a hand too small or too large.

## Knuckles form an arc
- The knuckles at the base of the fingers sit on a curve, not a straight line, and the fingers are longer than they look from the back. Check finger length against palm length in both images.
